Enemy incoming & send garrison

Enemy incoming rally

Type what the game shows and press Add. The countdown starts at once and your senders are called so they arrive just after impact. Editing a time moves the landing by the difference.

Send garrison / reinforcements

Send time = landing + arrive offset − march to garrison. "Next enemy hit" follows whichever enemy rally lands next. Pick a specific rally to reinforce between two hits, or "Own" to time it to one of your groups.

Rally leaders

Just names. Type one or several separated by commas. They appear as buttons on the Castle tiles map and under "From the Vault" in the timer.
